Solvent-Free Garment Restoration

Eco-Friendly Dry Cleaning & Textile Care

Traditional dry cleaning uses harsh perchloroethylene chemicals that break down delicate fibers and leave lingering smells. Our wet cleaning technology revitalizes your luxury garments with pure filtered water, biodegradable conditioners, and hand-finished steam pressing.

How Our Non-Toxic Process Works

Wet cleaning is an eco-safe alternative to dry cleaning developed in Germany. Utilizing computerized washing machines, extremely gentle mechanical action, and specialized bio-degradable soaps, it cleans delicate garments without degrading wool, silk, or synthetics.

1

Pre-Spotting & Stain Chemistry Diagnosis

We diagnose each stain under high-intensity daylight lighting. Natural enzymes dissolve tannin, grease, protein, and dye stains without bleaching or fiber damage.

2

Computerized Gentle Moisture Bath

Garments rotate in gentle oscillating cylinders at precision-calibrated temperatures (usually below 25°C) with lanolin-enriched fabric protective balms.

3

Moisture-Controlled Gentle Dehumidification

Infrared moisture sensors monitor fabric hydration continuously, halting the drying cycle at the precise threshold to prevent wool shrinkage or silk stiffness.

4

Artisanal Steam Pressing & Packaging

Trained tailors press each shoulder, lapel, cuff, and pleat on contoured buck forms. Items are draped onto sturdy wooden hangers inside breathable garment covers.
