Crafting Sustainable Garment Care

About FreshPress

We are on a mission to eliminate the toxic chemicals of traditional dry cleaning and give city dwellers their weekends back.

Our Founding Story

Born Out of Frustration with Lost Socks & Chemical Smells

Back in 2014, our founders Karthik and Rebecca were both busy city professionals. Every Saturday involved dragging heavy bags down flights of stairs, hoping the dry cleaner wouldn't scorch their suits or lose another button.

Worse still was the discovery that 85% of dry cleaners used Perchloroethylene—a hazardous solvent toxic to humans and harmful to waterways. We asked ourselves: Why can’t dry cleaning be completely non-toxic, digital, and collected directly from our door?

FreshPress began with one electric van and a passionate team of textile specialists. Today, we process over 25,000 garments a month across 15 metropolitan zones with zero harsh chemicals.

Our Core Values

The 3 Pillars of FreshPress Care

Every decision we make balances garment longevity, environmental stewardship, and customer convenience.

100% Toxin-Free Solvents

We use gentle water-based organic detergents and biodegradable enzyme conditioners that leave no chemical odor or residue on your skin.

Closed-Loop Water Recycling

Our industrial laundry plants recycle 65% of rinse water through multi-stage membrane filters, saving over 1.2 million gallons annually.

Zero-Emission Electric Fleet

100% of our daily customer pickups and deliveries are routed through zero-emission custom electric vans charged with renewable grid energy.
