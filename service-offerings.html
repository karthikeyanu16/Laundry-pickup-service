Strict Protocols

Our 5-Point Garment Inspection Guarantee

Every single piece that enters our treatment facility is photographed, weighed, and cataloged. We treat your wardrobe as if it were our own.

1
Pocket & Seam Search: Any forgotten items (pens, cufflinks, cash) are sealed in a tamper-proof envelope and returned.
2
Stain Diagnosis: High-definition photography and UV light analysis to detect invisible grease or protein spots.
3
Care Label Adherence: Zero guesswork. We follow manufacturer care symbols and test fiber composition before washing.
4
Complimentary Button Repairs: Loose buttons are hand-tightened and broken dress shirt buttons replaced free.
5
$1,000 Garment Protection Plan: All orders are covered against loss or manufacturing defects up to $1,000 per garment.
